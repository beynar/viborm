## Accepted baseline source contract

- `Commands.analyze(root)` creates one ordered occurrence history and private
  `analyzeInto` appends ordinary records, selected-series templates, and choice
  suffixes. This is the accepted baseline API.
- `RelationBody` creates one `SelectedSeries` with its provisional `analysis`
  and inserts `captureSeries` before later effects.
- `CommandExecution.captureSeries` publishes the parent when required, captures
  all rows, admits every actual member through `EngineSchema.member` or
  `EngineSchema.update`, constructs the existing `RecordCommand`, and stores the
  resulting members in `CommandAttempt.series` before execution.
- Each captured child currently enters `Commands.analyze(child)` as a fresh
  root. That is a statically confirmed boundary, not yet a reproduced cause.
  The G2.9 amendment supersedes this accepted fresh-history contract only if
  Unit 01 reproduces the stated enclosing-dependency failure.
- `EngineSchema.member(model, relation, source)` and `update(model, source, …)`
  are distinct admission scopes. They are not separate mutation languages.
- `OperationContext.prepareMembers` currently writes a `memberPath` onto each
  prepared member and keeps one operation-level `totalMembers`; committed
  member and segment counts remain operation-owned. `CommandAttempt` owns
  replaceable rows, bindings, choices, and captured series members.
- Construction, dependency analysis, and execution must share each
  occurrence's exact admitted values. Template and member scopes stay distinct,
  all captured members are prepared before the first member effect, and attempt
  replacement must not invoke another default, transform, or admission.

G2.9-02 supersedes only the accepted fresh-member-history rule after Unit 01
reproduced the enclosing dependency gap. The other baseline ownership rules
remain constraints on the repair.

| Case | Admission sequence | Required oracle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Template conflict | `wanted` | Dependency refusal before SQL; no member admission or effect |
| First member conflict | `other`, then `wanted` | Refusal after capture/admission but before any member effect |
| Later member conflict | `other`, then `second`, then `wanted` | Refusal before either member effect |
| Disjoint control | `other`, then `other2`; independent `wanted` row exists | Success; the intended sibling update targets the pre-existing `wanted` row, the series creates only `other2`, unrelated rows stay unchanged, and no false dependency is inferred |
