Why the results differ

A keyed invoice lookup can use direct index probes with sharing off. With sharing on, the measured late-match lookup scans 80,001 organizations to produce the shared set up to the matching key. Lists benefit because the same organization filter is built once instead of twice.

The bulk-update and single-relation controls emit identical SQL with either setting. Their timing differences are noise, not a sharing benefit.
